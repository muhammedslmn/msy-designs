Who we are

Passing on what was entrusted to us

Hidayah grew out of the wish not to keep the Islamic knowledge we learned to ourselves, but to pass it on and thereby serve Islam and the Muslims according to our means.

Our aim is to call people to Tawhid, to convey authentic Islamic knowledge in an understandable way, to clear up misconceptions and to strengthen Muslims in understanding, living and holding firm to their faith.
